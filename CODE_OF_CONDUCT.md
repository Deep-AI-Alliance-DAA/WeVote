# Code of Conduct

WeVote welcomes contributors with different backgrounds and levels of experience. Help make project discussions useful, respectful, and accessible.

## Expected conduct

Discuss ideas and code with care. Ask for context, explain disagreements, accept corrections, and give feedback about the work. Respect people's time, privacy, language, and accessibility needs. Credit others' contributions and follow licensing requirements.

## Unacceptable conduct

Harassment, discriminatory abuse, threats, personal attacks, sexual harassment, doxxing, and deliberate intimidation are not acceptable. Do not publish private contact details, credentials, participant records, or other confidential information without authorization. Repeated disruptive behavior, spam, and retaliation against people who report a concern are also not acceptable.

## Scope and reporting

This policy applies to repository issues, pull requests, discussions, and project spaces managed by maintainers. It also applies when someone represents the project in an official capacity.

Report concerns privately to maintainers through an available private channel. If none is listed, request a private channel without posting the report or identifying affected people in a public thread. Maintainers should protect the privacy of people involved as far as practical.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove content, request a correction, restrict participation, or temporarily/permanently exclude someone based on the conduct and its impact. When practical, they will explain the action privately and allow relevant context to be provided. Maintainers are expected to follow the same standards and avoid handling a report where they have a conflict of interest.
